# Excluded preliminary series

This series was interrupted during mixed_overload repetition 2, before the main
comparison. The pending-task guard of 256 was too close to the expected overload
backlog (~270 tasks). Only this generator safety ceiling was raised to 1024;
arrival schedules, upstream delay, budgets, retry rules and deadlines were unchanged.
The complete main series was restarted for every scenario/mode/repetition. These
preliminary data are retained for transparency and must not be pooled into main
results. The interrupted run may have incomplete records and lacks cleanup proof.
