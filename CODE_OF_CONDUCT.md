# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in project issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions
must treat others respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and experience
levels. Give specific, constructive feedback about the work, acknowledge mistakes,
and respect privacy and requests to disengage.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized behavior, personal attacks,
repeated disruption, impersonation and disclosure of another person's private
information are unacceptable. This applies to project spaces and to conduct
while representing this project elsewhere.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, issue a
warning, restrict participation or exclude a participant, proportionate to the
conduct and its repetition. Maintainers are also subject to these expectations.
Reports should be handled fairly, with the minimum disclosure needed to respond.

Report non-sensitive conduct concerns through a
[maintainer contact request](https://github.com/Xenox-developer/ai-protocol/issues/new?title=Maintainer%20contact%20request),
or contact the [project maintainer](https://github.com/Xenox-developer) through an
available profile contact route. If the matter is private, request a private
channel without posting incident details, identities or personal information.
Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive reports. The project is maintained
on a best-effort basis; no response-time guarantee is offered.
